  5. Un seul tir manqué termine la série.
  6. Mort subite : si personne ne marque deux manches de suite, la cage est vidée.
  7. À plusieurs, même cage : tes buts gênent les autres.
  8. En fin de manche, ceux qui ont manqué sortent. Sauf si tout le monde a manqué.

  1. You shoot on goal. Every goal you score plants a glove in front of the net — permanently.
  2. Three taps: width, height, then shoot.
  3. The cursor overshoots the posts. Aiming for the top corner leaves the net clearer.
  4. Shoot fast and the glove is small. Hesitate and it comes in large.
  5. A single miss ends your run.
  6. Sudden death: if nobody scores for two rounds, the net is cleared.
  7. In multiplayer you share one net — your goals get in everyone's way.
  8. At the end of a round, players who missed are out — unless everyone missed.
  9. Four keeper levels, from slow to razor-sharp.
  10. Your record is your longest run of goals.
